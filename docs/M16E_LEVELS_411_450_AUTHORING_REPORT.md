## Design and guidance departures

Locked titles and subjects are preserved. All forty boards lie within their approved per-level pixel and color bands. The 425 crescent, 445 arch and 450 beam repairs sharpen the approved silhouettes without changing their subjects. Dark voids are reserved for night, caverns, deep shadows and the 450 oculus; clouds, glass, reflections and the moonbeam use filled pixels. Productive route lengths below broad normal guidance were retained without padding. No palette, engine, global color hex, rendering algorithm or publication behavior changed.
